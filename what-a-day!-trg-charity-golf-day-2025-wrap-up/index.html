What a Day! TRG Charity Golf Day 2025 Wrap-Up

19 January 2026

If you’ve ever heard whispers that the Toowoomba Referral Group Charity Golf Day is something special, consider this your confirmation.

Our annual Charity Golf Day was nothing short of incredible. From the first tee-off to the final putt, the day was packed with laughs, generosity, community spirit, and (let’s be honest) a few creative golf swings.

The Big Result

Thanks to the overwhelming support of our sponsors, golfers, volunteers, and donors, we’re proud to share that we raised an incredible:

$32,803.70 for charity

Not only did we beat last year’s total, we absolutely smashed it.

All funds raised will be shared evenly between two very deserving charities:

  • Hope Horizons
  • Maddox’s Helping Hand Foundation

These funds will make a genuine, tangible difference in the lives of individuals and families in our community, and we couldn’t be more grateful to everyone who played a part.

More Than Just a Game of Golf

While the golf itself was… entertaining (accuracy was optional), the real highlight of the day was the atmosphere.

From generous sponsors and prize donors to volunteers who kept everything running smoothly, and golfers who brought the fun and the heart, the TRG community showed up in the best possible way.

It was a reminder of what can happen when local businesses and individuals come together with a shared purpose: to give back, support those who need it most, and enjoy the process along the way.

Thank You

To every sponsor, donor, golfer, volunteer, and supporter — thank you. Your generosity, energy, and willingness to get involved are what make this event bigger and better every year.

You are truly the best team we’ve ever teed up with.
