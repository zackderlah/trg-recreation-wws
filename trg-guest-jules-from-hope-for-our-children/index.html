TRG GUEST: Jules from Hope for Our Children

02 May 2026

A morning that pulled at our heartstrings

We were so honoured to have Jules Davey, CEO of Hope for Our Children, join us for a cuppa and a chat at our Wednesday morning meeting.

There wasn't a dry eye in the house as Jules spoke about the incredible work Hope for Our Children does, in particular, their Bags of Hope program.

Imagine being taken from the place you call home and sent somewhere unfamiliar, with nothing. That's exactly what Bags of Hope works to address. Providing vulnerable children with essential items to restore their dignity and inspire hope for a brighter future.

TRG was proud to donate $1,000 to this amazing cause. As a non-government funded organisation, Hope for Our Children relies entirely on grants and community support, so every single dollar truly counts.

If you'd like to learn more or support their work, visit hopeforourchildren.com.au.
