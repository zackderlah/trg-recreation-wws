Property Maintenance and Prevention

Hi, I’m Justin, owner and operator of Preventive Property Care. PPC is a mobile property maintenance service focused on helping homeowners, landlords and property managers keep their properties maintained, functional and in good condition. I handle a broad range of general property maintenance and smaller repair jobs, particularly the jobs that often don’t justify calling in a larger trade business.

  • Loose Hinges
  • Leaking Taps
  • Gaps
  • Worn Fittings

and other home issues - handled early while they're still simple and less costly.
