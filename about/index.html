About TRG

What We Do

Toowoomba Referral Group is a business referral group which focuses on providing business networking and business leads within a structured and positive environment.

Contact Us

What's Involved?

TRG is a registered Not for Profit Incorporated Association (IA55093) that organises business networking meetings every Wednesday from 9am to 10am for members and invited guests.

Each member is given the opportunity to promote their business. Guest and motivational speakers from a range of professions are regularly organised to present to the group.
