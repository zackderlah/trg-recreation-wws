TRG Annual Golf Day

The TRG Charity Golf Day is an annual community event hosted by the Toowoomba Referral Group to raise funds for two local charities.

Held at the picturesque Toowoomba Golf Club, it’s a day where local businesses, sponsors, and players come together for a round of golf, good food, and great company — all for a worthy cause.

With team registrations, hole sponsorships, and prizes throughout the day, the eventblends friendly competition with genuine community spirit, helping make adifference for local families affected by cancer and serious injury.

2026 Charities

Hope Horizons Cancer Wellness Centre opened its doors in Toowoomba, Southeast Queensland in 2017. Born from a desire to see free allied health services provided for those living with cancer in the region, while ever cancer remains, our mission is to diminish its impact.

Maddox's Helping Hand Foundation (MHHF) was established in 2020 and supports ACCISS where the public hospital system cannot. In May 2021 MHHF held its first fundraising event and has since raised almost $700,000.
