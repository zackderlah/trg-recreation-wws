Legal Services

At Briese Lawyers we offer a quality service that is based on our genuine care about the issues and relationships that matter to you.

In all matters and at all stages of a legal matter, we will put you, the client, first. In everything we do, we apply a values system of ethics and integrity. We aim to empower our clients with the highest quality legal advice that is practical, solution focused and tailed to meet the needs of our clients. We pride ourselves on a “can do” approach that gets results.

Established in February 2007, Briese Lawyers is a dynamic, young firm based in Toowoomba.

Our unique team of people have been carefully chosen to ensure the highest quality of legal service, professionalism and client care is offered toeach of our clients – we are in this together.

We offer the following areas of practice:

  • Family Law
  • Estate Planning & Administration
  • Residential Conveyancing
  • Commercial Law (Commercial Purchases and Sales, Leasing, Business Transactions)
  • Mediation

Margaret's background

“Margaret has been part of our Briese Lawyers family since 2009. She is enthusiastic and passionate about her role. In her time here Margaret has worked in the practice areas of Conveyancing and Administration.

Prior to joining our team, Margaret worked in sales, customer service and management. Margaret is currently employed as our Business Development & Relationship Manager. In her role Margaret works closely with our Principal, Kym Briese and our Office Manager, Madonna Wilson. She oversees our Administration Team and works closely with all members of the Briese Lawyers family as our Relationship Manager.

Margaret has a particular interest in marketing and she also enjoys working with many of the community groups and charities that our Firm raises funds and awareness for. In particular, Margaret enjoys working closely with Kym Briese in supporting Darling Downs Women in Business.

Margaret is a Commissioner for Declarations and lives on a farm in the Lockyer Valley with her husband and two children.”
